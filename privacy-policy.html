1. Introduction

HarpLegend is developed by ChewPacaStudios. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app on Android or iPhone, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

By using HarpLegend, you agree to the practices described here.

Android app: com.chewpacastudios.harphero (Google Play)

iOS app: com.chewpacastudios.harplegend (App Store)

Contact: chewpacastudios@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Microphone

HarpLegend uses your microphone to detect harmonica notes during gameplay and microphone calibration. This audio is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never recorded, stored, or transmitted
  • Only active while you play or calibrate

Notifications

HarpLegend may send you local notifications to support your practice habit:

  • Streak reminders — if you haven't played today and your streak is at risk
  • Inactivity reminders — after 3 or 7 days without playing
  • Streak milestones — when you reach a streak milestone (2, 4, 7, 10, 14, 21, 30, 45, and 60 days)
  • Weekly recap — a summary of your practice each Sunday
  • Memory review reminders — when a song you are memorizing in Memory Mode is due for review, at most one a day, and only for songs where you turned reminders on
  • App updates — when a newer version is available

All notifications are generated on your device using locally stored data. No personal data is sent to any server to produce them. You can disable individual types in Settings → Notifications (Memory Review reminders can also be turned off for each song), or disable all notifications in your device's system settings.

Account and Authentication

HarpLegend offers these ways to use the app:

  • Google Play Games (Android) — sign in with your Play Games profile. We receive a secure authentication token. Your Play Games display name is used to suggest a username.
  • Sign in with Apple (iPhone) — sign in with your Apple Account. Apple gives us a unique identifier and an email address, which may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". We do not ask Apple for your name, and we never use your email for marketing or show it to other players.
  • Guest account — use the app without signing in. An anonymous Firebase account is created. Your gameplay data stays on your device (see Premium below for the one exception).

We do not collect or store passwords. Your account is managed by Firebase Authentication.

Username

Signed-in players have a username. On Android it is generated automatically from your Play Games display name; on iPhone you choose your own nickname. Your username is:

  • Stored in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) to keep it unique across all players
  • Publicly visible on in-game leaderboards

Gameplay Statistics and Progress

For signed-in accounts, the following is synced to our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) so you can restore it on another device:

  • Songs played, play time, note accuracy stats, streaks, and streak shields
  • Play credits and challenge progress
  • Personal best score, accuracy, and combo per song, and songs you have unlocked
  • Memory Mode progress (review schedule and how many free Memory runs you have used)
  • Harmonica tuning calibration data (recorded frequencies per hole)
  • The number of songs you have uploaded (not the songs themselves)
  • Your Premium status

Guest accounts keep all of the above on the device only.

Feedback Survey (optional)

If you choose to answer an in-app survey, we store your answers in Firebase Firestore with your account, together with details that help us understand them: app version, device model, language, Premium status, gameplay statistics (songs played, active days, streaks, credits), and your pitch-detection settings. If you have consented to analytics, we also store your Firebase Analytics app instance ID so answers can be compared with app usage. Answering is voluntary; free players receive bonus credits whatever they answer. Guests cannot answer surveys. Survey answers are deleted when you delete your account.

Leaderboards

For signed-in accounts, your best score for each song is posted to a global leaderboard. The following is publicly visible to all players:

  • Username
  • Score, accuracy, and best combo per song

Guest accounts do not appear on leaderboards.

Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. It collects:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Session duration, screens viewed, and usage patterns
  • General location (country/region, derived from IP address)

We also send our own events:

  • Songs — song started, completed, or quit, with the song name, harp key, speed, mode, score, accuracy, grade, note counts, and duration
  • Premium — when the Premium screen is shown or closed, the plan you look at, its price and currency, whether a free trial is offered, and whether a purchase or restore succeeded, failed, or was cancelled
  • Rewarded ads and credits — when an ad is offered, requested, or completed, and when you run out of credits
  • Challenges and onboarding — challenges viewed, started, progressed, and completed, and when you finish onboarding
  • Feedback survey — when the survey is shown, opened, dismissed, or submitted
  • Whether you are a Premium player (as a user property)

Users in the EU, EEA, and UK are asked for explicit consent before analytics collection begins. Outside these regions, analytics is enabled by default. You can change your preference at any time in Settings → Manage Data Consent.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. It collects:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • A stack trace describing where the crash occurred
  • No name, email, or other directly identifying information

EU, EEA, and UK users are asked for explicit consent before crash reports are sent. Outside these regions, crash reporting is enabled by default. You can change this preference in Settings → Manage Data Consent.

Rewarded Ads (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource)

HarpLegend shows optional rewarded ads powered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). Watching an ad is always your choice — it earns you a free play credit. LevelPlay and its ad network partners may collect:

  • Device advertising identifiers — the Android Advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and coarse location (derived from IP)
  • Ad interaction data (views, completions, skips)

This data is used to serve and measure ads. On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency); if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared, and you can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. EU, EEA, and UK users are also asked for explicit consent before personalized ads are shown, and we do not ask for tracking if you decline. Outside these regions, personalized ads may be shown based on your device's ad settings. You can opt out of ad personalization at any time in Settings → Manage Data Consent, or on Android via Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Advertising data practices are governed by ironSource's Privacy Policy at ironsrc.com/privacypolicy.

Device Integrity (Android)

On Android, the app uses Google Play Integrity to check that it is running on a genuine, unmodified device. Google issues an integrity token, which our Cloud Function verifies; the result is used only to protect play credits from tampering and is not stored with your profile. A failed check is also recorded as an analytics event (if analytics is enabled).

Song Downloads

When you stream or download a song, the app requests a temporary download link from our Cloud Function using your account. Downloaded songs are stored on your device.

Profile and Local Data

The following is stored only on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Settings (volume, sensitivity, visual preferences, audio offset)
  • Profile picture
  • Songs you upload (MIDI and audio files)

Photo Library

If you set a profile picture, you choose one image from your photo library. That image is stored locally as a file and is never uploaded or transmitted.

3. Premium Subscriptions & Lifetime Purchase

HarpLegend offers three ways to unlock Premium, bought through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (which may start with a free trial), or a one-time "Lifetime Premium" purchase. You can buy as a guest or while signed in. In every case:

  • The store's proof of purchase — a Google Play purchase token, or an App Store signed transaction — is sent to our secure Firebase Cloud Function for server-side verification. We never receive or store your payment card details
  • On iPhone, each purchase carries an anonymous identifier derived from your account ID, so we can match it to your account
  • Your Premium status is stored in our cloud database (Firestore) linked to your account — active/expired with a renewal date for a subscription, or permanent for Lifetime Premium
  • We keep a purchase record (the store's transaction or purchase identifier, the product, and the accounts it has been linked to) so the purchase can be restored to the right account and cannot be claimed by someone else
  • Payment management (renewal, cancellation, refunds, billing history) is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple

You can cancel a monthly or yearly subscription at any time via Google Play → Subscriptions on Android, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. If your yearly subscription starts with a free trial, you can cancel during the trial and you will not be charged; otherwise it renews as a paid yearly subscription when the trial ends. Lifetime Premium is a single, non-recurring purchase that never expires and has nothing to cancel. If you buy as a guest and later sign in, your purchase follows you to your account.

6. Data Deletion

Delete Account (in Profile):

Permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account, username, all stats, scores, tuning data, Memory Mode progress, survey answers, and leaderboard entries. Local device data is also cleared. This action cannot be undone.

If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes HarpLegend's Sign in with Apple access. If that step cannot be completed, your account is still deleted and the app tells you how to remove HarpLegend in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Purchase records (see section 3) are kept after deletion so that a subscription or Lifetime Premium bought with your store account can still be restored. Deleting your HarpLegend account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

You may also visit tgaspe.github.io/harplegend/delete-account or contact us at chewpacastudios@gmail.com to request manual deletion.

7. Data Retention

  • Microphone audio — never retained
  • Firebase Analytics data — per Google's policies (typically 14 months)
  • Crash reports — per Google's policies (typically 90 days)
  • Firebase Auth account — until account deletion
  • Cloud gameplay data and username (Firestore) — until account deletion
  • Survey answers (Firestore) — until account deletion
  • Premium status (Firestore) — until account deletion; subscriptions also end at expiry, Lifetime Premium does not expire
  • Purchase records (Firestore) — kept after account deletion, to restore purchases and prevent fraud
  • Ad interaction data (LevelPlay) — per ironSource's data retention policies
  • Local device data — until app uninstall or account deletion
  • Guest session identifier — deleted on app uninstall

8. Children's Privacy

HarpLegend is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete the account and associated data.

10. Data Security

Local data is protected by the Android and iOS app sandboxes. Account credentials are managed by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us directly. Cloud data (Firestore) is protected by Firebase security rules — only you can read or modify your own data, and purchase records can only be written by our servers. No method of data transmission is 100% secure.
