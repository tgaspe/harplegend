How it works

  1. 1Follow the notes

    Notes scroll down ten lanes, one for each hole of the harmonica.

  2. 2Play them in time

    Blow, draw and bend as each note reaches the line. The microphone listens and scores every one.

  3. 3Climb the path

    A path of levels takes you from your first note to blues licks, with practice speeds and a memory mode.
